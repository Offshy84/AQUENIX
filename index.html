The refill concept

Simple idea. Fresh start.

The intended product experience is designed to make refilling straightforward. Final directions will depend on each product’s validated formulation.

01

Add water

Fill the reusable bottle with the specified amount of suitable water.

02

Add tablet or pod

Introduce the product-specific concentrated tablet or pod and allow it to dissolve as directed.

03

Use & refill

Use the prepared product according to its label instructions, then refill when needed.
