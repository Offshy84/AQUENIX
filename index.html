From concentrate to ready-to-use

How to prepare your liquid.

A simple preparation workflow for the future AQUENIX refill system. The exact water quantity, dissolving time and usage directions must be printed on each product label after formulation testing.

01 · Check the label

Choose the correct AQUENIX product and confirm its specified bottle size, water quantity and preparation instructions.

02 · Add measured water

Use clean water of the quality specified for that formulation. Pour the measured amount into the clean, reusable bottle.

03 · Add tablet or pod

Place the complete product-specific tablet or pod into the bottle. Do not combine different concentrates.

04 · Allow it to dissolve

Close only if the product directions permit, wait for the specified dissolving time, and mix gently if instructed.

05 · Check before use

Confirm the concentrate has dissolved and the liquid looks as expected. Follow any product-specific dilution or settling directions.

06 · Label the bottle

Identify the product and preparation date. Keep the original safety and usage instructions available.

07 · Use as directed

Apply only to the surfaces or uses stated on the product label. Observe any protective equipment and safety instructions.

08 · Store safely

Keep the prepared liquid in its labelled bottle, away from children, food and incompatible chemicals. Observe validated shelf life.

Development-stage note: These are general concept steps, not final product instructions. Do not publish fixed water volumes, mixing times, shelf life or performance claims until each AQUENIX formula has been tested and its directions approved. Never mix cleaning products or combine concentrates.
