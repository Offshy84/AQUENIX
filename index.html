The refill concept

Simple idea. Fresh start.

The intended experience is designed to make refilling straightforward. Final directions will depend on each product’s validated formulation.

01

Add water

Fill the reusable bottle with the specified amount of suitable water.

02

Add tablet or pod

Introduce the product-specific concentrated tablet or pod and allow it to dissolve as directed.

03

Use & refill

Use the prepared product according to its label instructions, then refill when needed.

From concentrate to ready-to-use

How to prepare your liquid.

A preparation workflow for the future AQUENIX refill system. Exact water quantity, dissolving time and usage directions must be printed on each product label after testing.

01 · Check the label

Confirm the product, bottle size, water quantity and preparation instructions.

02 · Add measured water

Use clean water of the quality specified for that formulation.

03 · Add tablet or pod

Place the complete product-specific tablet or pod into the bottle.

04 · Allow it to dissolve

Follow the product-specific dissolving and mixing directions.

05 · Check before use

Confirm the concentrate has dissolved and the liquid looks as expected.

06 · Label the bottle

Identify the product and preparation date.

07 · Use as directed

Apply only to the surfaces and uses stated on the product label.

08 · Store safely

Keep the prepared liquid in its labelled bottle and observe validated shelf life.

Development-stage note: These are general concept steps, not final product instructions. Do not publish fixed water volumes, mixing times, shelf life or performance claims until each formula has been tested. Never mix cleaning products or combine concentrates.
